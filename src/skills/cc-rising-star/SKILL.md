## Category Profile: Rising Star

Rising Stars are young, high-growth dividend initiators (e.g., early-stage dividend growers with strong CAGR).
They have higher volatility, rapid earnings growth, and significant price appreciation potential.
Covered calls should be used very selectively — the primary thesis is growth, not income.

## Adjusted Parameters

**All existing rules remain in force** — earnings gate, DTE ≤ 45 hard cap, fundamental quality checks, and all WAIT triggers. This skill ONLY adjusts thresholds within those rules.

### Delta Range
- **Target**: 0.10–0.20 (very conservative — well below default)
- **In strong uptrend**: 0.05–0.15 or WAIT entirely
- **Rationale**: Rising Stars can make large moves. Low delta preserves the growth thesis. You own these for appreciation, not income.

### Premium Thresholds
- **Minimum premium**: ≥ 0.8% of stock price for 30–45 DTE
- **Minimum annualized return**: ≥ 10%
- **WAIT threshold**: premium < 0.5% of stock price
- **Rationale**: Only sell calls when premium is worth the upside risk. Higher IV in these stocks naturally produces more premium at low delta.

### IV Requirements
- **IV Rank**: ≥ 40 preferred
- **WAIT if**: IV Rank < 25 — premium won't justify the upside cap
- **Rationale**: Rising Stars have naturally higher IV; a moderate bar ensures meaningful premium.

### Technical Adjustments
- **Strong uptrend (price > all MAs, rising momentum)**: WAIT — do NOT sell calls during strong momentum runs
- **Overbought (RSI > 75)**: Consider selling — potential pullback makes calls safer. Use delta 0.15–0.20.
- **Consolidation after a run**: Good opportunity — delta 0.15–0.20 above resistance
- **Pullback to support**: WAIT — let recovery play out

### Market State Guidance
When evaluating the current market state for this Rising Star:
- **Momentum (RSI > 70, near highs, accelerating)**: WAIT — protect the growth move
- **Overbought/Extended (RSI > 75, decelerating)**: Opportunistic sell at very low delta (0.10–0.15)
- **Neutral (RSI 45–65)**: Cautious sell — delta 0.15–0.20
- **Weak (RSI < 40)**: WAIT — premium is low AND you'd cap recovery
- **High IV spike (earnings, news)**: Best scenario — use delta 0.15–0.20 to capture elevated premium safely

### Ex-Dividend Consideration
- Minimal dividends (yield <2%) — ex-div risk is negligible for covered calls
- Focus on growth protection, not dividend timing

### Risk Profile
- **Primary risk**: Capping a stock that can move 20–30% in a year
- **Mitigation**: Very low delta, selective timing, WAIT when momentum is strong
- **Assignment outcome**: Significant opportunity cost — only acceptable at very high strikes
- **When NOT to sell calls**: During breakouts, around product launches, after strong earnings beats
