## Category Profile: Compounder

Compounders are quality growth stocks with moderate dividends (e.g., MSFT, AAPL, V, UNH).
Assignment means owning a strong compounder at a discount — a good outcome but timing matters.
Puts should target pullback entries in uptrending stocks.

## Adjusted Parameters

**All existing rules remain in force** — earnings gate, DTE ≤ 45 hard cap, investment worthiness check, and all WAIT triggers. This skill ONLY adjusts thresholds within those rules.

### Delta Range
- **Target**: -0.20 to -0.30 (standard)
- **On pullback to major support**: -0.25 to -0.35 (buy the dip)
- **Rationale**: Compounders appreciate — you want to own them, but at the right price. Standard delta with flexibility on dips.

### Premium Thresholds
- **Minimum premium**: ≥ 1.0% of strike price for 30–45 DTE (slightly reduced from default 1.5%)
- **Minimum annualized return**: ≥ 12% (reduced from default 18%)
- **WAIT threshold**: premium < 0.6% of strike price
- **Rationale**: Compounders have moderate IV — premiums are decent but not high. Slightly relaxed thresholds keep opportunities available.

### IV Requirements
- **IV Rank**: ≥ 30 preferred (reduced from default 50)
- **WAIT if**: IV Rank < 20 AND premium below minimum
- **Rationale**: Moderate IV is normal for quality compounders. Lower bar ensures opportunities on pullbacks.

### Technical Adjustments — Buy the Dip
- **Pullback in uptrend (price < 20MA but > 200MA)**: IDEAL setup for CSPs — sell puts at support
- **RSI < 45**: Good opportunity (relaxed from default RSI < 40)
- **At SMA 50 support**: Strong entry point for compounders
- **Below SMA 200**: Caution — verify the pullback is not a trend change

### Market State Guidance
When evaluating the current market state for this Compounder:
- **Oversold (RSI < 35)**: Excellent — compounders recover well from pullbacks. Full delta range.
- **Weakening (RSI 35–45)**: Good opportunity if near support levels
- **Neutral (RSI 45–60)**: Standard — only if at clear support
- **Overbought (RSI > 70)**: WAIT — stock is extended, pullback likely. Wait for better entry.
- **High IV (earnings, sector rotation)**: Good — capture elevated premium on quality stock

### Fundamental Quality
- Compounders have strong earnings growth (CAGR ≥10%) — quality is generally high
- Verify: earnings trend still positive, no margin compression, competitive position intact
- Low payout (≤50%) means dividend is very safe — focus on growth trajectory

### Support Level Selection
- SMA 50 is primary support for uptrending compounders
- Previous breakout levels become support
- Fibonacci retracements (38.2%, 50%) work well for pullback entries
- Target strikes at/below strongest support confluence

### Risk Profile
- **Primary risk**: Growth slowdown or sector de-rating
- **Assignment outcome**: Good — you own a quality compounder at a discount
- **Recovery expectation**: Compounders typically recover from pullbacks — be patient
- **Total return**: Premium + price appreciation + small dividend
