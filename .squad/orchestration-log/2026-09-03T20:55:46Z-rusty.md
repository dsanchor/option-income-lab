- Date window: today (UTC, inclusive) through _add_three_months(today) (inclusive)

- Deduplication key: (symbol, type, date); sort key: (date, symbol, type)
- `has_active_position` label appended when present
- Empty calendar: explicit "No earnings or ex-dividend events found for tracked symbols in the next 3 months." marker

- **File:** `backend/src/calendar_utils.py` (or equivalent)
- `_add_three_months(date)` uses `calendar.monthrange` for end-of-month clamping
- Jan 31 + 3 months = Apr 30 (not May 1 from 90-day approximation)
- Feb 28/29 clamping for non-leap years and leap years
