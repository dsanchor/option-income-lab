## Summary

**Defect diagnosed:** Buy Tracker output reported `Score 0/5` with all canonical dimensions unavailable (SMA50, SMA200, Stochastic K, dividend-growth-years missing). Full trace confirmed model, extraction, validation, and persistence all correct — bug upstream in market-data producer (`yfinance_data_provider.py`).

**Root causes (reproduced against real KO data):**
1. **Trailing incomplete session blanks rolling-window indicators:** yfinance's final row (today's session) often has `Close=NaN` due to data lag. Pandas rolling-window indicators (SMA, Stoch.K) propagate NaN, returning `None` (absence). EWM indicators (EMA, RSI, MACD) silently forward-fill the prior day — inconsistent behavior across indicator families.
2. **Partial current-year dividend bucket breaks streak:** Consecutive-growth loop resamples to annual totals; the in-progress current calendar year always compares as a decrease (fewer payments made), breaking the streak at `growth_years=0`.

**Fixes applied in `yfinance_data_provider.py` (data boundary, not shared calculator):**
1. New `_drop_incomplete_trailing_bars()` helper trims trailing rows with `Close=NaN` immediately after `ticker.history(period="1y")`, before any indicator computation. All indicator families (rolling + EWM) now anchor on the same last-genuinely-completed session. Interior gaps untouched.
2. `_build_dividends()` now excludes the still-forming current calendar year from growth-streak comparison (`annual.index[-1].year >= datetime.now(timezone.utc).year`) before counting consecutive years. Genuine cuts in *completed* years still break streak.
3. Defense-in-depth prompt clarification in `buy_tracker_instructions.py`: score_breakdown must always be emitted as a real 5-key object; missing data for one dimension only zeroes that dimension, never the whole object.
