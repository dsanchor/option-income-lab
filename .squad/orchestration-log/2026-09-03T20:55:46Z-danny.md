## Outcome

Designed the Buy Tracker six-state tri-state scoring contract. Specification accepted and validated by the team:
- **Six-state scale:** STRONG_BUY > BUY > ACCUMULATE > WAIT > UNFAVORABLE > AVOID
- **Tri-state dimensions:** -1 (headwind), 0 (neutral), +1 (tailwind)
- **Hard gates:** Dividend cut / triple bearish = hard AVOID; earnings ≤2d / RSI>80 / price extended = hard WAIT
- **Exceptional gate:** +5 score + full objective evidence + no hard gates → STRONG_BUY
- **Score format:** Signed representation (+3/5, -2/5, 0/5)
- **Alert policy:** STRONG_BUY, BUY, ACCUMULATE are alerting states; WAIT, UNFAVORABLE, AVOID are not
- **Missing data:** ≥3 missing dimensions → cap at WAIT with insufficient_data flag
- **Backward compatibility:** Old {0,1} breakdowns and unsigned score format remain valid
