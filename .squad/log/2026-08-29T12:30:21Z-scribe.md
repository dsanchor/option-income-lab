### Force Alpha

- **Gate:** `run_alpha = is_alert or prolonged_wait or force_alpha`

- **Cooldown-neutral (H1):** Forced reviews skip-over cooldown scan; don't reset due-wait timer
- **No force-only Telegram (H2):** Forcing never reaches alert/notification gates
- **Precedence:** Skip-reviews (buy_tracker) > incomplete_quote_wait (monitor) > force_alpha
- **Audit:** Every attempt persisted as `alpha_run = {"trigger", "forced", "status"}`
