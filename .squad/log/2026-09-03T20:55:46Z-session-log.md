## Risks Mitigated

1. **Buy Tracker state inflation:** New tri-state scoring with higher thresholds (BUY requires +4/+5) reduces false positives from ~95% to ~10–20%.
2. **Missing-data behavior:** Explicit handling of ≥3 missing dimensions caps at WAIT; prevents arbitrary states from incomplete evidence.
3. **Hard gates:** Dividend cuts and triple bearish market structure properly block entry signals.
4. **Calendar feature scope:** Portfolio-mode-only toggle with default-off prevents unintended inclusion in quick-analysis mode.
5. **Calendar arithmetic:** True month clamping (Jan 31 → Apr 30) instead of 90-day approximation ensures predictable date ranges.
